# Example Dental Care Ltd — changes to our invoices

Date: 15 September 2026
To: Accounts teams at our insurer partners

From 1 October 2026, our invoices will change in two ways.

First, invoice totals will exclude GST. GST will be shown on a separate line called "GST".

Second, the field "Provider No." will be renamed to "Provider ID". The number itself does not change.

Please update your systems before 1 October 2026. If you have questions, contact our accounts team.
